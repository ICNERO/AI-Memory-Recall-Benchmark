# License and third-party boundaries

The existing repository `LICENSE` is retained verbatim: **MIT License, Copyright (c) 2026 ICNERO**. Under the owner's publication instruction, this applies to the owner's original evaluator/baseline code, synthetic benchmark data, author annotations and documentation, to the extent the owner holds rights in them. No separate CC license is introduced.

Third-party works are not relicensed by this statement. The bundled o200k_base vocabulary and upstream tokenizer material retain the MIT notice in `vendor/TIKTOKEN_LICENSE.txt`. The pinned regex package is installed separately and retains its upstream distribution notices; no platform-specific binary/runtime from the full archive is republished here.

`sources/sources.jsonl`, `sources/source_mapping.jsonl` and `sources/topic_seeds.jsonl` restore the original bibliography, mappings and construction notes. Their historical access/verification dates are preserved; this publication has not independently revisited every source. The conversations include author-created explanatory prose, reference links and inherited citation markers. Source URLs are attribution/provenance, not evidence of a blanket redistribution grant. No complete external papers, textbooks or source-page downloads are added to this repository.

The publication check is a provenance and obvious-sensitive-data screening, **not a line-by-line copyright clearance or legal certification**. Third-party quoted/adapted expression, if any, remains subject to its own license and applicable requirements. Attribution alone does not establish reuse rights. The inherited historical notice in `vendor/INHERITED_LICENSE_NOTICE.md` is retained as a provenance record, not a claim that all original archive attachments are shipped here.

If a source-rights or privacy issue is identified, report the affected file and source location through a repository issue without reposting sensitive content. Changes to frozen text or evidence offsets require a new version and regenerated integrity checks; do not silently edit v0.5.2 data and retain its hashes.
